Welcome to the ATW800/2 disk

This disk is meant to get you started and have a quick check of the card's
basic functions. That is: Colo[u]r graphics and the virtual Transputer.

Due to the limited space on this disk, it mainly contains the xVDI driver
(booted through the AUTO folder) as well as the full installer for 
installing the driver onto your harddrive.

For a quick initial test of the virtual Transputer inside the "Seurat"
FPGA a fun little Mandelbrot fractal renderer was added.

The LINES.PRG will give you a quick impression about the speed and 
colours which you now have in your Atari system.

For more drivers, serious Transputer tools/OS and more please visit the
Software share which is linked on the ATW800/2 main page at:
  https://www.geekdot.com/atw800_2
(Scroll down for the "SOFTWARE" link)

Thank you for supporting this project and we hope you will enjoy
your ATW800/2!
